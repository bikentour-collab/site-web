Nantes Bike Tour: the Nantes panorama

The Château des ducs de Bretagne, the Elephant at Les Machines de l'île, the Voyage à Nantes artworks and the banks of the Loire – effortlessly by e-bike, with a guide who knows the city inside out.

In short. Nantes Bike Tour: the Nantes panorama: guided e-bike tour of Nantes by Bike'N Tour. Duration 2.5 hrs, €44 per person (E-bike and helmet, Passionate guide, Local treats, Annotated city map). Departures From Tuesday to Sunday at 3pm, in French, English or Spanish, maximum 12 people per guide, 100% on cycle paths. Meeting point: Bike'N Tour shop, 27 quai de Versailles (tram line 2, Saint-Mihiel stop).

Updated November 24, 2026 · Bike'N Tour, 27 quai de Versailles, 44000 Nantes

The route

  1. 1

    Bike'N Tour shop, quai de Versailles

    Briefing, bike fitting and an introduction to the route.

  2. 2

    Saint-Mihiel and the banks of the Erdre

    Quai de Versailles, the barges and the Japanese garden on Île de Versailles.

  3. 3

    Quai Ceineray and Cours des 50 Otages

    How water shaped Nantes, from the Vikings to the filling-in of the Loire's channels.

  4. 4

    Medieval quarter and Place du Bouffay

    The narrow streets of the old walled town.

  5. 5

    Château des ducs and Tour LU

    Is Nantes in Brittany? The life of Anne of Brittany, then the tasty story of LU biscuits.

  6. 6

    Île de Nantes

    The former shipyards, now a creative quarter, and the giant Elephant.

  7. 7

    Graslin, Passage Pommeraye and Place Royale

    The elegance of 19th-century Nantes, before heading back to the shop.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to ride an e-bike?

No: your guide will show you how it works before you set off, and the route is 100% on cycle paths.

Can I cancel?

Yes, the tour can be cancelled and refunded up to 48 hours in advance. To change the time, just get in touch: we always find a solution.

How many people per group?

Maximum 12 people per guide. For your own group (family, friends, colleagues), we run private tours at a time of your choice.

Included

  • E-bike and helmet
  • Passionate guide
  • Local treats
  • Annotated city map

Rendez-vous : Bike'N Tour shop, 27 quai de Versailles (tram line 2, Saint-Mihiel stop). Venez 10 minutes avant le départ.
