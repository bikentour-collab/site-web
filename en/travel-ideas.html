Cycle routes starting from Nantes

Six major cycle routes start right from our shop in the centre of Nantes: La Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante, the Brittany Crossing, the Nantes–Brest Canal and the Nantes Vineyards. Whichever you choose, we get your bike, panniers and GPX track ready.

In short. Six major cycle routes start from the Bike'N Tour shop at 19 rue Jean Jaurès in central Nantes: La Loire à Vélo (165 km, 3 to 4 days, easy level); La Vélodyssée (240 km, 4 to 5 days, easy level); La Régalante (275 km, 9 stages, easy level); The Brittany Crossing (485 km, 8 to 10 days, moderate level); The Nantes–Brest Canal (360 km, 6 to 8 days, easy level); The Nantes Vineyards (80 km, 2 days, easy level). For a first cycling trip or a family holiday, the easiest is the Loire à Vélo: flat, very well signposted and with train links for the journey back.

How do you choose your route?

For a first trip or with the family, the Loire à Vélo is the easiest: flat, very well signposted and with good train links for the journey back.

For the ocean, La Vélodyssée takes you to Pornic in two days and to the Vendée coast in four to five.

For Mont-Saint-Michel, La Régalante goes inland (275 km, hardly any climbing), while the Brittany Crossing follows the coast via Rennes and Saint-Malo (485 km).

For a weekend, try the Nantes Vineyards on a gravel bike or the Nantes–Brest Canal as far as Redon.
