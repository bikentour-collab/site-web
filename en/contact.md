## Our story

Bike'N Tour was born from a simple idea: Nantes sits at the crossroads of the Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée and the Breton cycle routes, yet it had no bike hire shop designed for cycle touring. Ten years on, the shop on quai de Versailles gets hundreds of travellers on the road every season, from all over France, the Netherlands, Germany and beyond.

We choose our bikes for cycle touring, several of them made just a few kilometres from Nantes by Sunn. We check them after every hire, and we take the time to show you the way. Our guides also take visitors around Nantes by e-bike, in small groups.
