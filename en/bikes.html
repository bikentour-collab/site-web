Our rental bikes in Nantes

Recent bikes, serviced after every hire: Velo de Ville touring bikes, Sunn e-bikes and gravel bikes made in Loire-Atlantique, and kids' bikes. All fitted to your size before you set off.

One-way hire (One Way)

Starting in Nantes and finishing your trip somewhere else? We arrange for the bikes to be collected at your destination, on the Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante or in Brittany, with our partners. Price on request, depending on your dates and drop-off point: get in touch in advance.

Damage and theft cover

En option : 2 € par jour (touring bike, kids' bike), 3 € par jour (e-bike, gravel bike). La casse est prise en charge à 100 % (hors usage abusif) et, en cas de vol, seuls 10 % de la valeur neuve restent à votre charge.

Included with every bike

  • Lock
  • Puncture repair kit and pump
  • Rear rack
  • Front and rear lights
  • Charger and battery key (e-bikes)
  • Bike fitted to your size and route advice
