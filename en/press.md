## Bike'N Tour in a nutshell

Bike'N Tour is a bike hire shop at 27 quai de Versailles, on the banks of the Erdre, opposite the Île de Versailles (tram line 2, Saint-Mihiel stop). The shop is open from April to October and holds the Accueil Vélo label.

- **4 types of bikes for hire:** touring bike, e-bike, gravel bike, kids' bike, with lock, puncture repair kit and lights.
- **6 major cycle routes from the shop:** La Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante, The Brittany Crossing, The Nantes–Brest Canal, The Nantes Vineyards.
- **3 guided e-bike tours of Nantes**, plus tailor-made rides for groups and companies.
