## The route

1. 1

   ### Tour de Bretagne

   Briefing, bike fitting and an introduction to the route.
2. 2

   ### Saint-Mihiel and the banks of the Erdre

   Quai de Versailles, the barges and the Japanese garden on Île de Versailles.
3. 3

   ### Quai Ceineray and Cours des 50 Otages

   How water shaped Nantes, from the Vikings to the filling-in of the Loire's channels.
4. 4

   ### Medieval quarter and Place du Bouffay

   The narrow streets of the old walled town.
5. 5

   ### Château des ducs and Tour LU

   Is Nantes in Brittany? The life of Anne of Brittany, then the tasty story of LU biscuits.
6. 6

   ### Île de Nantes

   The former shipyards, now a creative quarter, and the giant Elephant.
7. 7

   ### Graslin, Passage Pommeraye and Place Royale

   The elegance of 19th-century Nantes, before heading back to the Tour de Bretagne.

No: your guide will show you how it works before you set off, and the route is 100% on cycle paths.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, the tour can be cancelled and refunded up to 48 hours in advance. To change the time, just get in touch: we always find a solution.

### How many people per group?

Maximum 12 people per guide. For your own group (family, friends, colleagues), we run private tours at a time of your choice.
