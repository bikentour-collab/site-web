## How do you choose your route?

**For a first trip or with the family**, the [Loire à Vélo](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/loire-by-bike) is the easiest: flat, very well signposted and with good train links for the journey back.

**For the ocean**, [La Vélodyssée](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/velodyssee) takes you to Pornic in two days and to the Vendée coast in four to five.

**For Mont-Saint-Michel**, [La Régalante](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/la-regalante) goes inland (275 km, hardly any climbing), while the [Brittany Crossing](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/brittany-crossing) follows the coast via Rennes and Saint-Malo (485 km).

**For a weekend**, try the [Nantes Vineyards](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/nantes-vineyards) on a gravel bike or the [Nantes–Brest Canal](https://www.bikentour.com/en/travel-ideas/nantes-brest-canal) as far as Redon.
