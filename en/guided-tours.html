Guided e-bike tours of Nantes

Shaped by its port, Nantes has a thousand stories to tell. Our local guides share them with you by e-bike, in a small group and 100% on cycle paths: with family, friends or colleagues.

Why explore Nantes by e-bike?

A bike is the best way to discover Nantes: the historic centre, the Île de Nantes and the banks of the Loire are linked by cycle paths, and in two hours you see more than a whole day on foot would allow.

With electric assistance nobody struggles: teenagers, grandparents and keen cyclists all ride at the same pace. Your guide sets up your bike before you start and shows you how to use it.

Our guides live in Nantes. They tell the story of the city, from the Dukes of Brittany to the Machines de l'île, and share their favourite places so you can carry on exploring.

FAQ

How much does a guided bike tour of Nantes cost?

From €44 to €65 per person depending on the tour, with e-bike, helmet and guide included.

Are the tours suitable for families?

Yes: the route is 100% on cycle paths and the e-bikes even out any difference in fitness.
