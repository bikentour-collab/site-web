Wine & Bike Tour in Nantes

The city and the Nantes vineyards in one ride: the story of Muscadet and Loire wines, then a tasting of three wines and local cheeses by the river.

In short. Wine & Bike Tour in Nantes: guided e-bike tour of Nantes by Bike'N Tour. Duration 3.5 hrs, €65 per person (E-bike and helmet, Passionate guide, Tasting of 3 wines and local cheeses, Annotated city map). Departures From Thursday to Sunday at 2pm, in French or English, maximum 12 people per guide, 100% on cycle paths. Meeting point: Place de Bretagne, at the foot of the Tour de Bretagne (tram T3, Bretagne stop).

Updated November 5, 2026 · Bike'N Tour, 19 rue Jean Jaurès, 44000 Nantes

The route

  1. 1

    Tour de Bretagne

    Getting to grips with the bikes.

  2. 2

    Banks of the Erdre

    Quai de Versailles and Île de Versailles.

  3. 3

    Bouffay and Château des ducs

    The medieval town and the history of the wine trade.

  4. 4

    Île de Nantes

    The former shipyards and Les Machines de l'île.

  5. 5

    Tasting on the banks of the Loire

    Three wines from the Nantes vineyards and local cheeses, introduced by your guide.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to ride an e-bike?

No: your guide will show you how it works before you set off, and the route is 100% on cycle paths.

Can I cancel?

Yes, the tour can be cancelled and refunded up to 48 hours in advance. To change the time, just get in touch: we always find a solution.

How many people per group?

Maximum 12 people per guide. For your own group (family, friends, colleagues), we run private tours at a time of your choice.

Included

  • E-bike and helmet
  • Passionate guide
  • Tasting of 3 wines and local cheeses
  • Annotated city map

Rendez-vous : Place de Bretagne, at the foot of the Tour de Bretagne (tram T3, Bretagne stop). Venez 10 minutes avant le départ.
