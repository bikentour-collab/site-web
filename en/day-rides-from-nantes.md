# Bike rides around Nantes

A day or a weekend: our ideas for self-guided rides from the shop, with a TER train back when possible. Distances and levels are based on our own rides with the team.

[Book my bikes](https://bike-n-tour.lokki.rent)

![A cycling break in the centre of Nantes](https://www.bikentour.com/assets/img/v2-pause-nantes.webp)

**In short.** 11 ideas for day or weekend bike rides from the Bike'N Tour shop in Nantes: The Sèvre Nantaise to Clisson (37 km (one way)), The Loire banks to Oudon (30 km), The Loire à Vélo to Cordemais (46 km), Lac de Grand-Lieu (18 km), The Nantes panorama loop (20 km), The Erdre to Sucé-sur-Erdre (18 km), Mauves-sur-Loire (17.5 km), Le Pellerin via the south bank (24 km), Nantes – Pornic in two days (100 km), Nantes – Angers in two days (110 km), Nantes – Saint-Nazaire via the Vélidéale (90 km). Bike hire from €22 a day, e-bikes from €35.

Updated November 5, 2026 · Bike'N Tour, 19 rue Jean Jaurès, 44000 Nantes

## The Sèvre Nantaise to Clisson

Moderate

37 km (one way)Full day

The banks of the Sèvre, the Chaussée des Moines at Vertou, the Muscadet vineyards, then Clisson's medieval castle.

TER train back from Clisson

## The Loire banks to Oudon

Easy

30 kmFull day

One of the most beautiful stretches of the Loire à Vélo, via Mauves-sur-Loire and Le Cellier, almost entirely traffic-free.

TER train back from Oudon

## The Loire à Vélo to Cordemais

Moderate

46 kmFull day

The estuary marshes, herons and the Villa Cheminée, an artwork perched 15 metres up facing the river.

TER train back from Cordemais

## Lac de Grand-Lieu

Easy

18 kmFull day

From the Memorial to the Abolition of Slavery to the Maison du Lac at Bouaye, with an optional out-and-back to Pierre-Aigüe.

TER train back from Bouaye

## The Nantes panorama loop

Easy

20 kmHalf day

Follow Nantes' Green Line by bike: monuments, Voyage à Nantes artworks and the city's quirky corners.

Loop from the city centre

## The Erdre to Sucé-sur-Erdre

Easy

18 kmHalf day

“The most beautiful river in France”, according to King François I, with its châteaux and riverside guinguettes.

TER train back from Sucé-sur-Erdre

## Mauves-sur-Loire

Easy

17.5 kmHalf day

The riverside guinguettes of Sainte-Luce-sur-Loire and Mauves – ideal for families.

Back along the Divatte embankment or by TER train

## Le Pellerin via the south bank

Easy

24 kmFull day

Trentemoult, Rezé, then the marshes south of the estuary to the port of Le Pellerin.

Back by bike or by ferry to Couëron

## Nantes – Pornic in two days

Moderate

100 kmWeekend

The Estuaire trail, the Martinière canal, a night in Paimbœuf or Corsept, then the ocean at Saint-Brevin and the Côte de Jade.

TER train back from Pornic (40 min)

## Nantes – Angers in two days

Moderate

110 kmWeekend

Two stages of around 55 km on the Loire à Vélo, through Loire villages, vineyards and wetlands.

TER train back from Angers (40 min)

## Nantes – Saint-Nazaire via the Vélidéale

Easy

90 kmWeekend

The north side of the estuary through the marshes: The Settlers, the Villa Cheminée and the Lavau-sur-Loire observatory.

TER or TGV train back (40 min)
