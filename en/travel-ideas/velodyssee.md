# La Vélodyssée from Nantes

La Vélodyssée (EuroVelo 1) runs from Roscoff to Hendaye. From Nantes, it reaches the ocean along the Loire estuary, then heads down the Côte de Jade and the Vendée coast: marshes, beaches, pine forests and fishing ports.

## Suggested stages

A suggested breakdown starting from our shop – adapt it to your own pace. We'll talk it through with you when we fit your bike.

1. 1

   ### Nantes → Paimbœuf

   The Estuaire art trail, the Martinière canal and the fishermen's lift-net huts.
2. 2

   ### Paimbœuf → Pornic

   Saint-Brevin-les-Pins, the ocean at last, then the Côte de Jade to Pornic's old harbour.
3. 3

   ### Pornic → Saint-Jean-de-Monts

   The Bay of Bourgneuf and the Breton-Vendée marshes.
4. 4

   ### Saint-Jean-de-Monts → Les Sables-d'Olonne

   Pine-forest trails, Saint-Gilles-Croix-de-Vie and the finish at Les Sables.

## Don't miss

- The artworks of the Estuaire trail
- Pornic's old harbour
- The Breton-Vendée marshes
- The pine forest of the Côte de Lumière

## Getting back

Trains from Pornic to Nantes (40 min) and from Les Sables-d'Olonne to Nantes, or one-way hire on request.

## FAQ

### Can I cycle from Nantes to Pornic in a weekend?

Yes: 100 km in 2 days, with a night in Paimbœuf or Corsept, and a train back from Pornic to Nantes in about 40 minutes.

### Can I hire a bike one way for La Vélodyssée?

Yes, on request: you set off from our shop in Nantes and leave the bike at your destination. We arrange collection with our partners (quote on request).
