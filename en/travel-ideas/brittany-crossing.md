- The Guérande salt marshes
- The medieval town of Dinan
- Saint-Malo's walled city
- The bay of Mont-Saint-Michel

## Viewpoints along the route

- **La Roche-Bernard**A charming town perched above the Vilaine river and its harbour.
- **Barrage de la Rance**A tidal power station with views over the Rance estuary.
- **Cancale**The oyster port, with views across the bay of Mont-Saint-Michel.

## Getting back

Train back from Rennes or Saint-Malo, or one-way hire on request.

## FAQ

### How long does the Brittany Crossing take?

Allow 8 to 10 days for the 485 km, at 50 to 60 km a day. An e-bike is recommended for the hillier sections.

### Can I hire a bike one way for The Brittany Crossing?

Yes, on request: you set off from our shop in Nantes and leave the bike at your destination. We arrange collection with our partners (quote on request).
