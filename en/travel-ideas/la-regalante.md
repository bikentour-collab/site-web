# La Régalante, from Nantes to Mont-Saint-Michel

La Régalante is a recent cycle route linking Nantes to Mont-Saint-Michel through the heart of the countryside. Its 275 km, split into 9 stages, mostly follow greenways with very little climbing and pass through historic towns.

## Suggested stages

A suggested breakdown starting from our shop – adapt it to your own pace. We'll talk it through with you when we fit your bike.

1. 1

   ### Nantes → Nort-sur-Erdre

   The Erdre valley and its châteaux.
2. 2

   ### Nort-sur-Erdre → Châteaubriant

   Greenways and forests in the north of Loire-Atlantique.
3. 3

   ### Châteaubriant → Vitré

   Into Brittany and the medieval town of Vitré.
4. 4

   ### Vitré → Fougères

   Fougères castle, one of the largest in Europe.
5. 5

   ### Fougères → Mont-Saint-Michel

   The Normandy hedgerow country, then the finish facing the bay.

## Don't miss

- The Erdre valley

- The ramparts of Vitré and Fougères
- Arriving at Mont-Saint-Michel

## Viewpoints along the route

- **Sucé-sur-Erdre**The banks of the Erdre, with riverside cafés and châteaux.
- **Nort-sur-Erdre**A small port on the Erdre, where the Nantes–Brest Canal begins.
- **La Guerche-de-Bretagne**A charming small town with arcaded half-timbered houses.
- **Pontorson**The last stop before Mont-Saint-Michel, along the Couesnon valley.

## Getting back

Back by coach and train via Pontorson or Rennes, or one-way hire on request.

## FAQ

### Is La Régalante difficult?

No: it mostly follows greenways with little climbing. Allow 9 stages of 25 to 35 km on average, or fewer days if you ride further.

### Can I hire a bike one way for La Régalante?

Yes, on request: you set off from our shop in Nantes and leave the bike at your destination. We arrange collection with our partners (quote on request).
