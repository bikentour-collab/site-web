## Suggested stages

A suggested breakdown starting from our shop – adapt it to your own pace. We'll talk it through with you when we fit your bike.

1. 1

   ### Nantes → Nort-sur-Erdre

   The Erdre valley and its châteaux.
2. 2

   ### Nort-sur-Erdre → Châteaubriant

   Greenways and forests in the north of Loire-Atlantique.
3. 3

   ### Châteaubriant → Vitré

   Into Brittany and the medieval town of Vitré.
4. 4

   ### Vitré → Fougères

   Fougères castle, one of the largest in Europe.
5. 5

   The Normandy hedgerow country, then the finish facing the bay.

## FAQ

### Is La Régalante difficult?

No: it mostly follows greenways with little climbing. Allow 9 stages of 25 to 35 km on average, or fewer days if you ride further.

### Can I hire a bike one way for La Régalante?

Yes, on request: you set off from our shop in Nantes and leave the bike at your destination. We arrange collection with our partners (quote on request).
