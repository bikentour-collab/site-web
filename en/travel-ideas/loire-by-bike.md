# La Loire à Vélo from Nantes

La Loire à Vélo (EuroVelo 6) is France's most popular cycle route. From Nantes, it follows the river upstream to Angers and Saumur on a flat, very well signposted route that is largely traffic-free: the ideal trip for your first cycling holiday, with family or friends.

## Suggested stages

A suggested breakdown starting from our shop – adapt it to your own pace. We'll talk it through with you when we fit your bike.

1. 1

   ### Nantes → Ancenis

   Out of Nantes along the Loire, past Mauves-sur-Loire and its riverside guinguettes, and Oudon with its tower.
2. 2

   ### Ancenis → Angers

   Champtoceaux, the hillsides of the Layon and Anjou, then Angers and its castle.
3. 3

   ### Angers → Saumur

   Tufa-stone villages, cave dwellings and a finish at the foot of Saumur castle.

## Don't miss

- The Château des ducs de Bretagne at the start
- The riverside guinguettes of Mauves-sur-Loire
- Angers castle and the Apocalypse Tapestry
- The cave dwellings and Saumur castle

## Getting back

Direct trains from Saumur and Angers to Nantes (TER, bikes accepted depending on the season), or one-way hire on request.

## FAQ

### How many days does it take to cycle the Loire à Vélo from Nantes to Saumur?

Allow 3 to 4 days for the 165 km between Nantes and Saumur, at 40 to 60 km a day. The Nantes – Angers weekend (110 km) takes 2 days.

### Is the Loire à Vélo suitable for children?

Yes: the route is flat and often traffic-free. With children, plan on 20 to 30 km a day; we hire out kids' bikes, baby seats and trailers.

### Can I hire a bike one way for La Loire à Vélo?

Yes, on request: you set off from our shop in Nantes and leave the bike at your destination. We arrange collection with our partners (quote on request).
