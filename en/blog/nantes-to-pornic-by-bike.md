# Pornic by bike: from Nantes on the Vélodyssée

Nantes to Pornic by bike: 100 km on the Vélodyssée over two days, via the Loire estuary and the Côte de Jade, with a TER train back. An accessible weekend of cycle touring, starting from our shop in the centre of Nantes.

![Pornic by bike: from Nantes on the Vélodyssée](https://www.bikentour.com/assets/img/blog-pornic-a-velo-1.webp)

From Nantes to Pornic, the Vélodyssée runs down the Loire estuary to the ocean, then follows the Côte de Jade to Pornic's old harbour. Allow about 100 km, best done over two days so you can enjoy the scenery. It's an ideal first cycling trip: flat terrain, continuous signposting and a direct TER train back. The start is our shop, right in the centre of Nantes.

## The route

### Stage 1: from Nantes to Le Pellerin

You leave Nantes along the south bank of the Loire. From Nantes to Saint-Brevin-les-Pins, the Vélodyssée shares its route with La Loire à Vélo. Take care as you ride through the city: traffic is heavy and the signs are sometimes hard to spot. After Rezé and Bouguenais, cycle paths and quiet roads lead to Le Pellerin, about 24 km from the start.

### Stage 2: from Le Pellerin to Saint-Brevin-les-Pins

This is the wildest section. You follow the Canal de la Martinière, completely flat, through the estuary's wetlands, passing through Frossay. You then reach Paimbœuf, once the outer port of Nantes, followed by Corsept and Saint-Brevin-les-Pins. This section of about 37 km, on quiet country roads and cycle paths, is rated easy and suitable for families. Take care when crossing the main roads.

For a weekend, people generally stay the night in Paimbœuf or Corsept, which splits the route into two balanced days.

### Stage 3: from Saint-Brevin-les-Pins to Pornic

The last 40 kilometres follow the Côte de Jade through Saint-Michel-Chef-Chef and Préfailles, partly on a greenway. About three quarters of this section is on shared roads: in summer, traffic is heavier, so stay alert. You arrive in Pornic along the coast, between coves and fishing huts.

## What to see along the way

- **The Canal de la Martinière:** a 19th-century canal running parallel to the Loire, through the Massereau and Migron nature reserves. A peaceful, car-free stretch.
- **Paimbœuf:** its colourful façades along the quays and its neo-Byzantine Saint-Louis church.
- **The Serpent d'océan:** in Saint-Brevin-les-Pins, this giant sea serpent skeleton lying on the foreshore is an artwork from the Estuaire trail.
- **The fishing huts of the Côte de Jade:** these fishing cabins on stilts dot the coastline between Saint-Brevin-les-Pins and Pornic.
- **Pornic's old harbour and castle:** the fishing harbour empties almost completely at low tide. The medieval castle above it can be admired from the outside.

## Practical information

### Getting back by train

Pornic is the terminus of a TER line that reaches Nantes in about an hour, via Sainte-Pazanne and Bouaye. Bikes are accepted subject to available space, which is in high demand in summer: travel outside peak hours if you can. On request, we also offer one-way rental.

### Exploring Pornic on foot and by bike

Around Pornic, the coastal customs path (GR 8) follows the shore for 14 km within the town's boundaries. It's a hiking trail: lock up your bike and explore it on foot. For riding, Destination Pornic offers cycling loops and cycle paths across its 15 towns and villages, from Saint-Michel-Chef-Chef to Les Moutiers-en-Retz.

### Taking a break and staying overnight

The villages along the estuary and the coast have shops and café terraces. In summer, book your overnight accommodation in advance.

### When to go?

From April to October, while our shop is open. Outside July and August, the coastal roads are quieter and bike spaces on trains are easier to find.

## FAQ

### Can you ride from Nantes to Pornic in one day?

It's possible for trained cyclists, but 100 km in a day leaves little time for sightseeing. Two days let you enjoy the estuary and the coast.

### Is the Vélodyssée between Nantes and Pornic difficult?

No: the route is almost flat. The difficulty lies mainly in the distance, the wind and the summer traffic on the coastal roads.

### How do you get back from Pornic to Nantes with your bike?

By TER train, with your bike on board subject to available space. Allow about an hour's journey to Nantes.
