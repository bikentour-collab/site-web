E-bike hire in Nantes: why try one?

Hire an e-bike in Nantes to try it out: how it works, real-world range, who it suits and our tips for riding well. An e-bike opens up the city and its surroundings to everyone, and hiring one is the best way to find out if it's right for you.

Trying an e-bike in Nantes means discovering that you can explore the city and its surroundings without arriving out of breath, whatever your level. A day's hire or a guided tour lets you test one in real conditions, before setting off on a cycling trip or buying one. Here's how it works, what you need to know about range, and our tips as a bike rental shop.

How does an e-bike work?

An electrically assisted bike (e-bike) is still a bike: the motor only works when you pedal. It amplifies your effort, it doesn't replace it.

In France, an e-bike must meet specific rules:

  • Power: a motor with a maximum continuous rated power of 250 W.
  • Speed: the assistance cuts out at 25 km/h. Above that, you can keep pedalling, but on leg power alone.
  • No throttle: the motor is activated by pedalling and cannot run on its own.

So you can ride one without a licence or registration, and it uses cycle lanes just like a regular bike. Helmets are not compulsory for adults, but they are strongly recommended.

The assistance modes

A small display on the handlebars lets you choose the level of assistance. Eco mode is light and economical. The most powerful modes give you a real boost on climbs or into the wind, but drain the battery faster.

Why try an e-bike in Nantes?

To smooth out the hills and the wind

Nantes is fairly flat, but it has its climbs: rue Crébillon, rue Jean-Jacques Rousseau or the Butte Sainte-Anne. With the assistance, they're effortless. Along the Loire, it's often the headwind that wears you out: the motor makes up for it.

To go further

With an e-bike, a trip to Mauves-sur-Loire, Sucé-sur-Erdre or the Vignoble nantais becomes an easy day out. Nantes Métropole has more than 845 km of cycling infrastructure, and it was recognised as a "Destination France à vélo" in October 2026: there's plenty of ground to cover.

To ride together, whatever your level

As a couple, with friends or with the family, an e-bike evens out differences in fitness. Someone who rarely cycles can easily keep up with someone who rides a lot.

Who is an e-bike for?

  • People who haven't cycled for a long time and want to get back into it gently.
  • Couples or groups with different levels.
  • Cycle tourers carrying panniers or planning long stages.
  • Regular cyclists who want to find out whether an e-bike would suit them day to day, before buying.

What range can you really expect?

Range depends on many factors. Manufacturers mention in particular:

  • the assistance mode: this is the most important factor;
  • the total weight: rider, bike and luggage;
  • hills and wind;
  • temperature: the battery performs less well in cold weather;
  • tyre pressure and road surface;
  • the way you ride: speed, starts, gear changes.

Our e-bike, the Sunn Start S1, is fitted with a Bosch Active Line Plus motor and a 400 Wh frame battery. It offers around 100 km of range in eco mode. The battery can be removed with the key provided, so you can charge it where you're staying.

Our tips for getting started

  • Set off in eco mode and in a low gear: the start is smoother and you save battery.
  • Change gear as you would on a regular bike: using your gears well improves range. Shift down before you stop.
  • Save the powerful modes for climbs or the end of a stage.
  • Charge it every evening when touring, even if the battery isn't empty.
  • Brake early: with luggage, the bike is heavier than a regular bike.

FAQ

Do you need to be sporty to ride an e-bike?

No. You do have to pedal, but the assistance greatly reduces the effort. All you need is to know how to ride a bike.

What is the range of a Bike'N Tour rental e-bike?

Around 100 km in eco mode, with the Bosch motor and the 400 Wh battery. It decreases in powerful mode, with luggage, wind or hills.

Can you go on a cycling trip with an e-bike hired in Nantes?

Yes. Our e-bike has a luggage rack, and the removable battery can be charged where you're staying. It suits the Loire à Vélo as well as hillier routes.
