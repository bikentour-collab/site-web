Bike accessories for hire in Nantes

Waterproof Ortlieb panniers, handlebar bag, phone holder, saddle cover, helmet, baby seat and trailer: everything you need to set off by bike, to add to your hire.

Our accessories for hire

Accessories are booked together with your bike and are waiting for you at the shop, fitted and adjusted before you set off: panniers attached to the rack, phone holder installed, helmet adjusted. For a multi-day trip on the Loire à Vélo or the Vélodyssée, the pair of Ortlieb rear panniers (2 × 20 L) is enough for two people travelling light; the handlebar bag keeps papers, phone and snacks within reach. Everything is returned to the shop at the end, or at your destination if you choose a one-way hire.

FAQ

Can I hire accessories without a bike?

Accessories are hired together with a Bike'N Tour bike. If you have your own bike, give us a call and we'll look at what's possible together.

Do the panniers fit every bike?

Yes, the Ortlieb panniers fit on the rear rack of our touring bikes and e-bikes.

How do I book accessories?

At the same time as your bike, on our booking site, or by phone.
