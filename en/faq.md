# Hiring a bike in Nantes: all your questions

Hire, booking, deposit, delivery, getting to the shop: answers to the questions we're asked most often. Another question? Call us on [06 52 62 94 64](tel:+33652629464). For the full picture on one page: [our guide to bike hire in Nantes](https://www.bikentour.com/en/bike-rental-nantes-guide).

## Bike hire

### Which bikes can I hire from Bike'N Tour in Nantes?

Velo de Ville touring bikes, Sunn e-bikes with Bosch motors, Sunn gravel bikes, kids' bikes from 20 to 26 inches and a Babboe electric cargo bike. A baby seat and trailer are available as extras.

### Can I hire a bike for several days or several weeks?

Yes – by the day, the weekend, the week or longer. Rates get cheaper the longer you ride: for example, €110 a week for a touring bike and €172 for an e-bike.

### Which accessories are included?

Lock, puncture repair kit, pump, rear rack and lights, plus the charger and battery key for e-bikes. Ortlieb panniers, handlebar bag, helmet, phone holder, baby seat and trailer are available as extras.

### What range do the e-bikes have?

Around 100 km in eco mode, thanks to the Bosch motor and 400 Wh battery. The battery comes out with the key provided, so you can recharge it where you're staying.

### Are the bikes suitable for cycle touring and bikepacking?

Yes: sturdy frames, hydraulic disc brakes, reinforced tyres and Shimano drivetrains, designed to carry panniers on mixed surfaces.

## Booking and services

### How do I book a bike?

Online in a few clicks, by phone on 06 52 62 94 64 or by email at contact@bikentour.com. We confirm your booking within 24 hours.

### Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before you set off. If something unexpected comes up, contact us as soon as possible: we always look for a solution.

### Do you deliver bikes to hotels or the station?

Yes, anywhere in the Nantes metropolitan area: hotel, holiday cottage, Airbnb or Nantes station. Delivery costs €10 (up to 4 bikes).

### Can I return the bikes in another town?

Yes, on request: we arrange for the bikes to be collected at your destination (Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante, Brittany) with our partners (quote on request).

## Deposit, damage and assistance

### What deposit do you ask for?

€500 for a touring or kids' bike, €1,000 for a gravel bike, an e-bike or a cargo bike, capped at €2,000 for several bikes.

### What happens if the bike is damaged or stolen?

Damage and theft cover is available as an option (€2 a day for a touring or kids' bike, €3 for an e-bike or gravel bike). It covers 100% of damage (excluding misuse), and in the event of theft you only pay 10% of the bike's new value.

### Is there assistance during my trip?

Yes, phone assistance 7 days a week. If an on-the-spot repair is needed, we'll give you a quote or help you find a bike repairer nearby.
