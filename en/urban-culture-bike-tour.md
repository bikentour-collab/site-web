## FAQ

### Do I need to know how to ride an e-bike?

No: your guide will show you how it works before you set off, and the route is 100% on cycle paths.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, the tour can be cancelled and refunded up to 48 hours in advance. To change the time, just get in touch: we always find a solution.

### How many people per group?

Maximum 12 people per guide. For your own group (family, friends, colleagues), we run private tours at a time of your choice.
