Hiring a bike in Nantes: the complete guide

Prices, choosing a bike, routes from Nantes, one-way hire, delivery, deposit: everything you need to know before hiring a bike for a day ride or a cycling holiday, from the Bike'N Tour team.

In short. To hire a bike in Nantes for a cycling trip, Bike'N Tour (19 rue Jean Jaurès, city centre) offers touring bikes from €22, e-bikes from €35, gravel bikes from €35, kids' bikes from €20 and cargo bikes from €50 a day, with delivery for €10, one-way hire on request and free cancellation up to 48 hours before. Six cycle routes start from the shop, including the Loire à Vélo (165 km to Saumur).

Updated November 3, 2026 · Bike'N Tour, 19 rue Jean Jaurès, 44000 Nantes

Where can I hire a touring bike in Nantes?

At Bike'N Tour, 19 rue Jean Jaurès, 44000 Nantes, inside the Ibis Nantes Centre Tour Bretagne hotel. The shop is right in the city centre, served by tram T3 (Jean Jaurès stop) and close to the Bretagne car park. It is open from April to October, Tuesday to Sunday (every day in July and August), 9.30am–12pm and 2pm–6.30pm. Six major cycle routes start from here: La Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante, the Brittany Crossing, the Nantes–Brest Canal and the Nantes Vineyards.

How much does bike hire cost in Nantes?

At Bike'N Tour, day hire costs €20 for the kids' bike, €22 for the touring bike, €35 for the e-bike, €35 for the gravel bike and €50 for the cargo bike. Prices drop for longer hires: a touring bike costs €110 for the week, an e-bike €172.

Which bike should I choose for my trip?

A touring bike is all you need for flat routes like the Loire à Vélo or La Vélodyssée. An e-bike is recommended for long stages, hilly routes like the Brittany Crossing, and couples with different fitness levels. A gravel bike suits the compacted-gravel paths of the Nantes Vineyards and the Nantes–Brest Canal.

Which cycle routes start from Nantes?

Six cycle routes start from the centre of Nantes, from 80 km (the Nantes Vineyards loop) to 485 km (the Brittany Crossing to Mont-Saint-Michel). The Loire à Vélo to Saumur (165 km, 3 to 4 days) is the easiest for a first trip.

What's included in the hire?

Every bike comes with lock, puncture repair kit and pump, rear rack, front and rear lights, charger and battery key (e-bikes) and bike fitted to your size and route advice. On request, Bike'N Tour also emails you the route sheet and GPX track.

Optional extras: Pair of waterproof Ortlieb rear panniers (2 × 20 L) (€5 a day), handlebar bag (€5 a day), comfort saddle cover (€1 a day), phone holder (€1 a day), baby seat (from 1 year) (€5 a day), child trailer (from 1 year) (€15 a day) and helmet (€1 a day).

Can I return the bike in another town (one-way hire)?

Yes: Bike'N Tour arranges for the bikes to be collected at your destination, on the Loire à Vélo, La Vélodyssée, La Régalante or in Brittany, with its partners. The price is quoted on request, depending on your dates and drop-off point, so it's best to ask in advance.

One-way hire in detail

Can the bikes be delivered to my hotel or the station?

Yes, Bike'N Tour delivers and collects bikes anywhere in the Nantes metropolitan area (hotel, holiday cottage, rental or Nantes station) for €10 per delivery, for up to 4 bikes.

What about the deposit and insurance?

The deposit is 500 € (touring bike, kids' bike), 1,000 € (e-bike, gravel bike, cargo bike), capped at €2,000 for several bikes. Optional damage and theft cover is available: €2 a day (touring bike, kids' bike), €3 a day (e-bike, gravel bike).

How do I book, and can I cancel?

You can book online at bike-n-tour.lokki.rent, by phone on 06 52 62 94 64 or at the shop. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before you set off. You can pay online or at the shop, by credit card or cheque.

Can I take a guided bike tour of Nantes?

Yes: Bike'N Tour offers three guided e-bike tours, in small groups of up to 12 people and 100% on cycle paths: Nantes Bike Tour: the Nantes panorama (2.5 hrs, €44 per person); Wine & Bike Tour in Nantes (3.5 hrs, €65 per person) and “Urban Culture” e-bike tour (2.5 hrs, €45 per person).

All guided tours

What about groups or companies?

Bike'N Tour organises outings for groups and company events: hire from €16.67 excl. VAT per bike, private guided tours from €36 excl. VAT per person, team building on request, and 10% off for 10 people or more.
